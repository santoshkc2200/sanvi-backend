# Tenant data-processing terms — draft for completion

This document is a drafting aid, not executed terms. Complete the schedules, obtain legal review for the applicable jurisdictions and processing, and execute the agreement with each tenant before relying on it. Do not describe a draft as a signed DPA.

1. **Parties and roles.** Identify Sanvi's registered legal entity and address and the tenant legal entity and address. The tenant determines purposes and means for its customer data; Sanvi processes that data on documented instructions. Separately identify any platform-account processing for which Sanvi is controller. Prohibit health data and unsupported regulated uses, and define the 16+ admission rule and treatment of known violations.
2. **Processing schedule.** List the service, duration, instructions, purposes, data types, subject categories, hosting/support locations, retention, deletion/return choice, and authorized contacts. Cover backups, logs, media, exports, support access and external processors; do not use an unbounded "all data" description.
3. **Instructions and confidentiality.** Limit processing to documented lawful instructions, including transfers. Require confidentiality and appropriate access controls for personnel. Define how Sanvi flags an instruction it believes conflicts with applicable data-protection law and handles legally compelled disclosures.
4. **Security schedule.** Attach actual technical and organizational measures, incident escalation contacts, access management, encryption/key management, tenant isolation, audit logging, recovery, retention and verification evidence. Avoid warranties about controls that are not deployed.
5. **Subprocessors.** Attach the current named list with purpose, location and safeguards. Define authorization, advance change notice, objection/resolution process and equivalent processing duties. Sanvi remains responsible as required by the applicable agreement/law.
6. **Rights and assistance.** Define authenticated request routing, assistance with access, portability, correction, restriction, objection, deletion, complaints, regulator inquiries and impact assessments. Set a short processor response SLA that allows the controller to meet its deadline. State required retained-data exceptions and provider limitations transparently.
7. **Incidents.** Require notification to the tenant without undue delay after awareness, continuing updates, available scope and impact information, preservation of evidence, mitigation assistance and coordinated communications. Do not condition the first notice on a completed investigation or encryption alone.
8. **Return and deletion.** Define termination export, secure deletion, justified retention and backup expiry. Require durable erasure replay before restored data is used again. Record downstream deletion and retained exceptions; specify the external notification retention arrangement and any manual rights handling.
9. **Assurance and audit.** Provide information needed to demonstrate compliance, proportionate audit/inspection rights, remediation cooperation and a clear allocation of costs that does not defeat required rights.
10. **International transfers.** Attach the reviewed, executed instruments and schedules for actual transfers (including onward transfers and remote access). Determine EU SCC modules, UK addendum/IDTA or valid alternatives, and Japan foreign-transfer information/safeguards as applicable. Complete importer/exporter details, measures, assessments and representative contacts; do not assume US registration provides an adequacy mechanism.
11. **Local terms and precedence.** Add applicable US service-provider/contractor restrictions and other local processor requirements, conflict/precedence clauses, governing law and competent authorities. Review future commerce separately for merchant identity, consumer terms, taxes, subscriptions, cancellation/refunds and accessibility.

Execution schedule: parties, effective date, processing schedule, security schedule, subprocessor schedule, transfer instruments, authorized contacts and signatures remain to be completed.
